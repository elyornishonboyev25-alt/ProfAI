# Academic Skills article expansion

Curated on 2026-10-04: 200 additional English reading articles; the four existing articles retain their IDs, slugs and content.

## Selection

- Source: [Frontiers for Young Minds](https://kids.frontiersin.org/articles), a science publication written for young readers.
- Reviewed a catalog of 1,987 candidate articles, screened for readable text and source glossaries, then selected concrete topics about animals, nature, everyday technology, sport, friendship, learning and the senses.
- Omitted adult/distressing topics and specialist clinical subjects. Selected text has an estimated Flesch–Kincaid grade of at most 11, with an average of 9.0. This is an English readability estimate, not a CEFR assessment or a promise that every age can read independently.
- Articles contain 686–1839 words, including headings, and 1141 glossary entries in total. Definitions come from the original source; examples come from the displayed article text.
- Category totals: Learning 12, Psychology 32, Science 149, Society 7.

## Attribution and adaptations

Each production record stores the original URL, full author names, citation, copyright notice and CC BY 4.0 link. These credits appear in the reader. [The publisher explains educational reuse here](https://www.frontiersin.org/news/2017/07/05/frontiers-for-young-minds-using-frontiers-for-young-minds-articles-in-your-classroom).

The text-only editions keep the main prose, section headings and relevant lists. Illustrations and their pointers, inline reference markers, bibliography and publication administration sections are omitted. Glossaries appear in the existing vocabulary panel and activity routes. Articles that depend on absent figures, tables, supplementary boxes or audio/video were excluded. Each article carries an adaptation notice and a link to its illustrated original.

The additions reuse ArticleCover, Articles and ArticleReader, along with existing bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading progress and vocabulary activities. No remote images are required for the reading experience.

## Validation

`npm run validate:articles` verifies unique IDs, slugs, titles, bodies and source URLs; licensed attribution; readable-length content; glossary integrity; and the absence of missing illustration pointers.

`npm run test:articles-ui` exercises the shared catalog/cards, search, category filtering, reader text, credit links, saved highlights/bookmarks, reader themes and vocabulary routes.

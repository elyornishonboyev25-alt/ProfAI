# Speaking Full Mocks 21–30

The existing Speaking bank had 30 topics, cue cards and discussion themes per part: the first 10 are reserved for practice days and indices 10–29 power Full Mocks 1–20. Ten new sets per part were written for Full Mocks 21–30, with distinct prompts and model answers. These are original IELTS-style practice prompts, not claims of past exam questions.

Each mock has a familiar Part 1 topic, one Part 2 long-turn card with four bullet points, and a linked Part 3 discussion theme. The structure follows the [official IELTS Academic Speaking sample and test format](https://www.ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test).

`npm run validate:speaking` checks all 40 sets per part for unique IDs and duplicate normalized question prompts, and requires a sample answer for every item. The catalog reserves new indices 30–39 for Full Mocks 21–30 without changing the existing Day 1–30 or Full Mock 1–20 assignments.

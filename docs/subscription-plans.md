# IvyGoo plans and access

The public plans are Free, IvyGoo Pro ($5.99/month) and IvyGoo Elite ($11.99/month). Existing period prices, group discounts and payment callbacks are preserved. MAX remains the internal Elite product/audience/tier identifier so existing purchases keep working. Student and Teacher subscriptions retain their legacy mapping; administrator and permanent grants remain unmetered.

Free includes the first five entries in each visible IELTS Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking full-test catalog, the first five IELTS full mocks and the first five SAT mocks (including their section practice). These are permanent catalog entitlements, not five attempts or five user-selected tests per month. Reopening or retaking them does not consume an allowance. The first Reading tests use the regrouped roadmap IDs; their original passages and the individual tasks of the first five Writing tests share that access. Other catalog tests and mocks require either paid plan.

Test vocabulary follows the corresponding test, including direct activity links and the query-based vocabulary reader. Articles, podcasts, shadowing, personal vocabulary and saved reviews stay free. Existing ResourceUnlock and historical test counters do not grant access to paid catalog entries.

Text AI renews on the first day of each UTC month: Free 50, Pro 300 and Elite 1500 successful requests. The interface presents only the remaining percentage, starting at 100%; raw message counts are not advertised. Failed requests refund the originally charged period, including a request spanning a month boundary. Writing/Speaking assessment quotas remain separate (2/30/150 for each skill).

Live AI is paid-only and separate from text AI balance. Pro receives 1 hour per UTC day and 30 hours per UTC month; Elite receives 4 hours per UTC day and 120 hours per UTC month. Time is stored in seconds. Both WebRTC and fallback voice reserve time in a database transaction, reject parallel sessions, and release unused seconds exactly once at hangup. Failed provider connections refund the reservation. The call deadline is bounded by remaining daily/monthly time, midnight, subscription expiry and the configured per-session connection duration. Reloading does not renew the deadline. Abandoned sessions retain their reservation; a backend reaper hangs up expired provider calls after restart. Voice tool replies and fallback voice delivery do not debit text AI balance.

Deploy frontend and backend together. The backend startup runs pending Prisma migrations, including 20261031090000_voice_time_allowances. That migration creates durable voice reservations and archives older session-count usage under voice_legacy_sessions rather than interpreting session counts as seconds. Do not reset existing AI usage counters during deployment.

Validation: npm run build; npm --prefix backend run test:billing; npm run test:billing-ui. Browser layout checks use mocked billing responses; no paid AI provider calls are needed to validate entitlements or quota accounting.

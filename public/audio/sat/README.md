# SAT break nature recording

`quiet-nature.mp3` is a real stereo field recording by **lastraindrop**, used under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- [Original recording and licence](https://freesound.org/people/lastraindrop/sounds/744133/)
- Original title: *Flowing water in canal after a storm and insect chirping at mignight*
- Published: 1 July 2024
- Public audio source: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/744/744133_12768873-hq.mp3
- Content: flowing water, distant birds and insects recorded at night in Guilin, China.
- Duration: 12 minutes 20 seconds, covering the complete 10-minute SAT break.
- Preparation: loudness normalised to approximately -20 LUFS with a -3 dBTP ceiling,
  short fades at the recording boundaries, stereo MP3 at 48 kHz / 160 kbps.

The recording streams from this application's own origin only after Play is pressed.
There is no video, third-party player, external audio request, or automatic playback.
